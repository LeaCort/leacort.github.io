Secret Rules

A party game of hidden rules. One phone, 3 to 20 players, ages 7 and up, nothing to set up.

Download on the App Store

How it works

Each round, everyone secretly receives the same behaviour rule, except one person: the Guesser. For five minutes the conversation carries on as if nothing were happening. The Guesser watches, asks questions and looks for what the others have in common.

Made for the table

  • One phone for the whole group, no account, no sign-up.
  • Works offline. Nothing you type ever leaves the phone.
  • 40 rules in the free edition, suitable for families, classrooms and friends.
  • English, French, Spanish, German and Italian.
